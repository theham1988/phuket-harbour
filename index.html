FAQ

Common Questions

Is hotel pickup really free?

Yes, completely free for all bookings regardless of your hotel location. We cover all of Phuket — Patong, Kata, Karon, Nai Harn, Rawai, Bang Tao, Surin, and everywhere in between.

What happens if the boat is cancelled due to weather?

If a departure is cancelled for safety reasons, we offer a full refund or a free reschedule — your choice. We message you on WhatsApp as early as possible.

How far in advance should I book?

We can often accommodate same-day bookings, but for popular dates in high season (December–March) we recommend booking at least 2–3 days ahead.

Is online payment safe?

Yes. We use Omise, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway. Your card details are encrypted and never stored on our systems. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PromptPay QR.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Reschedules are free with 24 hours notice. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before departure receive a full refund. Within 24 hours, we offer a credit for a future trip.

Do children get a discount?

Yes — children aged 2–10 pay 50% of the adult price. Children under 2 travel free. We will need their ages when booking to prepare the right life jackets and seats.

What should I bring on the tour?

Swimwear, sunscreen (reef-safe preferred), a hat, and a waterproof bag for your phone. We provide snorkelling gear, life jackets, drinking water, and fruit on board.

What pier do departures leave from?

All routes depart from Rassada Pier (Phuket Town Pier). Your hotel pickup brings you directly there — no separate transport needed.
